A security and IT company from Nattika, Thrissur

Alert LookLive designs, installs and supports CCTV, access control, networking and IT systems for homes, businesses and institutions.

Who we are

We are a local technology and security business. Our office is at Pink Tower on Nattika Beach Road, and most of our work is in Nattika, Thrissur and the towns around them. We also take on projects elsewhere in Kerala when the requirement suits.

What we do

Our work covers CCTV and surveillance, access control, gate automation, networking and IT infrastructure, servers and storage and communication systems. Many customers need more than one of these, and having one team for all of them keeps the job simple.

How we approach a project

  • Understand the premises. We ask what you want to protect or connect, and look at the site where we can.
  • Recommend what fits. The system should match the size of the property, the budget and how you will use it.
  • Install cleanly. Cabling, mounting and configuration are done to be tidy and easy to maintain.
  • Stay available. Maintenance, upgrades and relocation are part of the service.

Our aim

We want customers to feel safer and to find their IT dependable. That means listening to what they need, choosing technology that suits it, and supporting it after the installation is done.
