Privacy policy

How Alert LookLive handles the information you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, optional email address, the location of your site, the service you are asking about and your message. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, we receive whatever you send.

How we use it

We use these details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and arrange any site visit. We do not sell your details.

WhatsApp and other services

If you choose WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms. This website also shows an embedded Google map, which Google may serve with its own cookies when the map loads.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently run analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, this page will be updated.

Your choices

To ask us to correct or delete details you have sent, email info@alertlooklive.in.
