Request a free site visit

We don’t publish fixed prices online, because the right system depends on your site. A free visit is the most accurate way to get a quotation.

How it works

  1. You tell us a bit about your site

    Use the form below, call or WhatsApp us. A few lines about the property and what worries you is enough to start.

  2. We visit and look properly

    We look at the layout, entrances, cable routes and anything already installed, in Nattika, Thrissur and the towns around them, and elsewhere in Kerala for larger projects.

  3. You get a quotation, not a guess

    Based on the visit, we quote for the cameras, cabling and equipment your site actually needs, with no obligation to go ahead.
