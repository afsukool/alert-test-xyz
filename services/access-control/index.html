Access control systems for offices, institutions and businesses

Control who can enter which doors, and keep a clear picture of your entrances by pairing access control with CCTV.

Request a free site visit Call +91 8086 446 819 WhatsApp

What we install

  • Door access systems for offices, campuses and business premises
  • Card and RFID readers
  • Biometric readers
  • Access control planned together with your CCTV at key entrances

What we need to know

To recommend a system we ask how many doors need control, who needs access to which areas, and whether the doors are new or existing. Tell us what you want to restrict and we will suggest a practical way to do it.

Good fit for

  • Offices with restricted rooms such as server rooms or records
  • Schools and colleges with staff-only areas
  • Shops and small businesses with stock rooms or back offices
  • Commercial buildings with multiple tenants

Pairing readers with cameras at the same doors means you can see who was there as well as who was allowed in. See our CCTV services for the camera side, or smart home and light automation to have entrance lights respond to the same activity.

Where we work

We are based in Nattika, Thrissur, and take on access control projects in nearby towns and across Kerala, depending on the requirement. See our service areas or contact us to check your location.

Questions about access control

Do you provide access control installation?

Yes. We install door access systems for offices, institutions and businesses. You can ask about card/RFID and biometric options on our access control page.

Do you serve locations outside Nattika?

Yes. We regularly work in Thrissur city and the towns around Nattika, and we take on projects elsewhere in Kerala depending on the requirement. Tell us the location when you contact us and we will confirm what is practical.
