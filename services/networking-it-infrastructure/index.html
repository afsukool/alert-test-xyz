Structured cabling

Cabling is the part of a network that is hardest to change later. We plan cable routes, network points and racks so that new computers, cameras, phones and access points can be added without redoing the building. Neat, labelled cabling also makes faults faster to find.

Why networking and CCTV go together

IP cameras, access control readers and IP phones all depend on the network. Planning the network and the security system together avoids overloaded links and repeated cable work.

Good fit for

  • Offices setting up or moving premises
  • Colleges and schools that need wired and Wi-Fi coverage
  • Shops and small businesses that need a dependable connection for billing and cameras

Networking often goes with servers and storage and communication systems.
