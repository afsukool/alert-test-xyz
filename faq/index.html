CCTV and IT installation: your questions answered

Straight answers on cost, remote viewing, upgrades, networking and where we work.

How much does CCTV installation cost in Kerala?

We don’t publish fixed prices, because the right system and its cost depend on your site: how many cameras you need, the camera type and resolution, how many days of recording you want to keep, cable lengths, and whether the site is a home, shop or larger premises. The most accurate way to get a number is a free site visit. If you’d like to think it through first, try our CCTV package builder and send us what you have in mind.

How much does a CCTV system cost for a house?

A house with a few cameras at the entrances and boundary needs a much smaller system than a multi-storey home or a property with several buildings. Rather than quote blind, we look at the areas you want covered and the recording and remote viewing you need, at a free site visit. You can also start by building your own package to see the options.

Do you offer a free site visit before I decide?

Yes. For most homes and businesses we can visit, look at the layout and cable routes, and give you a quotation based on what we see, at no cost. Request a free site visit and we will get in touch to arrange a time.

Can I view my CCTV cameras from my phone?

Yes. Most modern IP, NVR and DVR systems support remote viewing on a smartphone or computer, as long as the premises has an internet connection. We set this up during installation and show you how to use it.

Do you provide CCTV maintenance?

Yes. Cameras need cleaning, connections need checking and recorders need updates and enough storage. We support the systems we install, and you can contact us about an existing system that needs attention. Details of our CCTV services are on the service page.

What is the difference between IP and analog CCTV?

Analog (HD) cameras send video to a DVR over coaxial cable and are often the more economical choice for straightforward setups. IP cameras send digital video over a network cable to an NVR and generally offer higher resolution, more flexible placement and easier remote access. We recommend one or the other based on your site and budget.

Can you upgrade an existing CCTV system?

Often, yes. Depending on the condition of the existing cables, cameras and recorder, we can replace only the parts that need it, add cameras, or move the system to a new location. We have reinstalled an existing CCTV system when a client moved offices. Contact us with what you have and we will advise.
